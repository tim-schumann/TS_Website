Blob

Is it a cell, a bacteria, or some thoughts swirring around? However, they want to be united, but the do not know how! Help them unite themselves before time runs out while guiding them with your mouse or finger!

Guide the liquid blobs with your cursor or touch. Fuse them into one cluster to trigger the blackout explosion and advance to the next level.

Survive as long as you can; if the timer runs out you can always retry.
